09Research

Measure it. Model it. Then decide.

An A+ undergraduate thesis in machining, three years of supply chain decisions, and the areas I want to research next.

Regression model: cutting speed, feed and depth of cut predict cutting force and temperature.

  • Cutting speed v
  • Feed f
  • Depth of cut d

Regression
model

  • Cutting force F
  • Temperature T

Why dry? Cutting fluids add cost, disposal and health concerns. Dry cutting removes them but runs hotter and harder on the tool, and surface textures are one way to manage that.

Undergraduate thesis · BUET, Industrial & Production Engineering · Graded A+

Micro-textured cutting tools for dry turning of medium carbon steel

Supervised by Dr. Prianka Binte Zaman

  1. MakeLinear grooves cut by wire EDM and micro-dimples by sinker EDM, with the equipment available in Bangladesh.
  2. Cut dryTurning medium carbon steel with no cutting fluid.
  3. MeasureCutting force and temperature for each tool and setting.
  4. ModelA regression model predicting both from cutting speed, feed and depth of cut.
  5. DecideThe linear-dimple tool performed best.

Best setting for the linear-dimple tool: cutting speed 85.17 metres per minute, feed 0.103 millimetres per revolution, depth of cut 0.5 millimetres.
